Round robin of the surviving 1993 CoreWar programs

This is not the original competition data of the First Hungarian Memory War (CoreWar) Championship of 1993. Some programs here were never entries at all. The results come from a new tournament played in 2026 with Viktor's 1993 MARS.COM under DOSBox.
